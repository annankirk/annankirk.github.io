My primary research interest is language variation and change. I’m most interested in discourse-pragmatic variation, syntactic variation, and syntactic change. The problem that keeps me up at night is where variation lives in a minimalist model of the grammar. I have a healthy amount of interest in "p-side" linguistic variation, corpus linguistics, discourse analysis, and [queer linguistics](https://www.lsadc.org/Files/committees/RevisedTrifold-What%20is%20Queer%20and%20Trans%20Linguistics.pdf). I’m also interested in language and emotion (thanks to a fascinating class taught by [Rachel Olsen](https://academics.siu.edu/humanities-social-sciences/linguistics/faculty/olsen-rachel.php) at SIU), TESOL, and second language pronunciation & identity.
