I'm a 4th year PhD student at Michigan State University researching linguistics. I'm a member of the Sociolinguistics Lab [website](https://sociolab.msu.edu/) and a research assistant for the MI Diaries project [website](https://mi-diaries.org/meet-the-team/). I'm currently leading and developing our new Participant Engagement Team: designing ways to keep our project participants feeling like part of a community and telling quality stories.

### How I talk
This section is under construction! I hope to build a "my idiolect" section soon, inspired by Dr. Joey Stanley's [page](https://joeystanley.com/pages/idiolect/). 

The tldr is that I speak a variety of North American English influenced by my time growing up in Texas. Vowels-wise, I'm pen-pin merged, cot-caught merged, and I have a little bit of PRICE monopthongization. I do not have double modals, but I support them.
