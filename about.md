## Annan Kirk

I'm a 4th year PhD student at Michigan State University researching linguistics. I'm a member of the MSU [Sociolinguistics Lab](https://sociolab.msu.edu/) and a research assistant for the [MI Diaries project](https://mi-diaries.org/meet-the-team/). I'm currently leading and developing MI Diaries's new Participant Engagement Team: designing ways to keep our project participants feeling like part of a community and telling quality stories.

I earned an MA in Linguistics from Southern Illinois University, and I earned a BA in Liberal Studies (with concentrations in Linguistics, Spanish, and Psychology) from Stephen F. Austin State University. 

### How I talk
I grew up in a small town a couple hours south of Dallas, Texas. Vowels-wise, I'm pen-pin merged, cot-caught merged, and I have a little bit of PRICE monopthongization. I do not have double modals, but I think they're swell.
