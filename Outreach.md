As a research assistant for the [MI Diaries project](https://mi-diaries.org/meet-the-team/), I'm currently leading and developing our new Participant Engagement Team: designing ways to keep our project participants feeling like part of a community and telling quality stories. The bulk of the work is curating themed weekly questions designed to elicit personal narratives and differentiated for adults, teens, and kids. 

## Community outreach through MI Diaries
- (Ongoing) Support for Hannah Community Center 100 year celebration event. Interviewed community members to build a special collection of audio diaries for feature at celebration.
- [MSU Science Festival](https://mi-diaries.org/2025/05/08/12873/) MI Diaries booth. Invited visitors to participate in two dialect games and consider the ways sociolinguistics connects to their lives. Led by Dr. Betsy Sneller and Dr. Suzanne Wagner. [2024](https://www.instagram.com/p/C6nWDDUuwhx/?utm_source=ig_embed&utm_campaign=loading), [2025](https://mi-diaries.org/2025/05/08/12873/), 2026.
- 2024 MSU Grandparents University.


## Other community outreach
- 2022, October 4. [World Languages Day](https://news.siu.edu/2022/10/100322-SIU-to-host-about-900-students-for-World-Languages-Day-2022.php). Southern Illinois University. Hosted local high school students for a day of special experiences focusing on world languages, cultures, and how language works. Planned, staffed, and executed a World Language Escape Room.
- 2022-2023. Rainbow Cafe LGBTQ Center, Carbondale IL. Event planning and youth programming for local LGBTQ+ youth. Served on board of directors and grant monitoring committee.
