At NWAV 53, I presented work from my first PhD qualifying paper, titled **‘really’ is really frequent: intensifiers and change in Michigan English**. I analyzed intensifiers of adjectives (e.g. **really** good, **super** cool) in the MI Diaries corpus and found that the overall distribution matched that of the intensifiers in Toronto English 20 years ago: in apparent time, **really** appeared to be increasing in relative frequency, and **very** appeared to be decreasing.
